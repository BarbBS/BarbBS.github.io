We use intraday data to measure volatility, forecast risk and trace how markets absorb information.

## Main questions

Does realized volatility from intraday data forecast risk better than GARCH and stochastic volatility models? Is intraday data useful for Value-at-Risk forecasts? How do European stock indices react, minute by minute, to US macroeconomic announcements?

## Methods

Realized volatility and jump measures, GARCH-type and stochastic volatility models, Value-at-Risk backtesting and event studies on intraday returns.
