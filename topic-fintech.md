We study how fintech firms develop, how they interact with banks and how digital assets and artificial intelligence change financial risk and regulation. Much of this work comes from the COST Action CA19130 Fintech and Artificial Intelligence in Finance (FinAI).

## Main questions

What drives and what holds back the fintech sector in Poland? Did the pandemic change the relationship between fintechs and banks? Which risks do digital assets carry, and how should regulation respond? How can statistical and fuzzy-logic methods extract patterns from peer-to-peer lending data?
