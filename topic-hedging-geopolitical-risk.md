We test whether gold, cryptocurrencies, commodities and other asset classes act as hedges or safe havens when markets face geopolitical conflict, pandemic stress or energy-price shocks.

## Main questions

Which asset classes hedged the Russian invasion of Ukraine, and do the answers hold across other conflicts? Is there one safe haven for different kinds of turbulence? Do cryptocurrencies hedge oil price fluctuations, and do proof-of-work and proof-of-stake coins differ as hedges against green and dirty energy? When does an apparent safe haven turn out to be a false one?

## Methods

Event-based and time-varying hedge and safe-haven tests, dynamic conditional correlations, volatility spillovers and portfolio strategies with volatility targeting.
