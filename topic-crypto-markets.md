We study how cryptocurrency markets price risk, how shocks spread across coins and how liquidity behaves in a market that trades around the clock.

## Main questions

Which characteristics explain the cross-section of cryptocurrency returns, and does machine learning improve on linear models? Do uncertainty and geopolitical risk enter cryptocurrency prices? Which coins lead the market, and has Bitcoin lost its central position in the return network? Can we predict which coins will become "zombie" assets before they fade from trading?

## Methods

Cross-sectional asset-pricing tests, machine-learning classifiers and return predictors, dynamic return and spillover networks, causality tests and intraday microstructure analysis.
