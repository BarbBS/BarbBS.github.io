Journal editors shape what a discipline publishes. We map the editorial boards of finance journals, measure their gender composition and analyse the network formed by editors who sit on several boards.

## Key findings

Women account for about 20% of editors of finance journals, with notable differences across countries.

Editorial affiliations are heavily concentrated in the United States and the United Kingdom.

Highly connected editors, who serve on many boards, show the same gender ratio as the whole population: men consistently outnumber women.
